Project Overview

The business problem, objectives, and analytical solution.

Business Problem

A global airline group needed a unified way to monitor operational reliability, revenue efficiency, cost structure, and customer experience across ten airline identities. Data was scattered across flight operations, disruptions, customer feedback, bookings, and cost ledgers — with no single source of truth for executive decision-making.

Objectives

  • Build a 16-table star-schema semantic model from curated flight, booking, cost, disruption, and feedback data.
  • Define 54 governed DAX measures across flight operations, revenue, costs, and customer experience.
  • Deliver five executive Power BI report pages with 120 visuals.
  • Validate everything with 609 automated tests (595 core + 14 canonicalization) and 34/34 manifest hash verification.
  • Record and enforce governance decisions (OD-04g, GOV-D-04 through GOV-D-08, D-02, CC-01, OD-01A, OD-01B) and gate approvals (G3-RV-01, schema freeze, G3, G4) — all closed 08 November 2026.

Solution

A reproducible Python data pipeline generates and validates synthetic datasets, Power Query M loads them into the Power BI semantic model, DAX measures compute KPIs, and five report pages deliver executive insight — all under a strict "synthetic simulation" disclosure.

Analytical Use Cases

Executive Oversight

Monitor flights operated, revenue passengers, on-time performance, cancellation rate, and total disruptions at a glance.

Operations Reliability

Analyze on-time departure/arrival, flight completion, block minutes, disruption categories, and airline operational comparison.

Revenue & Yield

Track operating revenue, passenger and ancillary revenue, revenue by airline/channel/fare class, and monthly trends.

Cost Intelligence

Break down operating costs by category, airline, and route; compute cost per flight operation and cost per revenue passenger.

Customer Experience

Measure satisfaction scores, complaint rates, feedback channels, service recovery, and customer segmentation.

Technology Stack

Tools, languages, and platforms used — each with its actual role in the project.

Python

Data processing, ETL automation, data-quality validation, and test orchestration. Deterministic random seeds for reproducible generation.

Pandas

Data cleaning and manipulation — curating flight, booking, cost, disruption, and feedback datasets into analysis-ready frames.

NumPy

Numerical operations and array processing supporting the data-generation and validation pipeline.

Power BI

Interactive executive dashboards — five report pages, 120 visuals, semantic model, and DAX measure layer.

DAX

Business calculations and KPIs — 54 governed measures (FL, OP, DS, CF, RV, CX, NC groups) registered in the semantic model.

Power Query M

Data transformation — 16 queries loading and shaping curated CSV/Parquet data into the Power BI model.

Microsoft Excel

Financial analysis and reporting — 12-sheet workbook with KPIs, data dictionary, and data-quality checks.

CSV + Parquet

Data storage - 16 curated data files (dimensions and facts) exported as UTF-8 CSV and Snappy Parquet.

Data Engineering

Pipeline components, curation, and validation logic.

FactFlightOperations

Deterministic generation of 5,000 flight operation records with realistic schedules, delays, and cancellation patterns. Uses seeded randomness for reproducibility.

  • 5,000 flight legs across 10 airline identities
  • Block minutes, departure/arrival times, on-time performance
  • Delay categories: late, very late, cancelled

FactBookingTransactions

9,956 booking coupons generated with fare class assignments, channel attribution, and passenger segmentation. Supports revenue and channel analysis.

  • 9,956 booking coupons
  • Fare class distribution (Economy, Premium, Business)
  • Booking channel: Direct, OTA, Corporate

FactOperatingCosts

7,480 cost lines categorized by type (fuel, maintenance, crew, landing, etc.) and airline. Supports cost per flight and cost per passenger calculations.

  • 7,480 cost lines
  • Cost categories: Fuel, Maintenance, Crew, Landing, Other
  • Airline attribution

FactDisruptions

1,754 disruption events recorded with categories (delay, cancellation, diversion), airline, route, and timestamp. Supports on-time performance calculations and delay analysis.

  • 1,754 disruption events
  • Categories: Delay, Cancellation, Diversion
  • Airline and route attribution

FactCustomerFeedback

2,500 feedback events collected across channels (email, phone, survey, app) with type classification (complaint, compliment, claim, survey), satisfaction score (1–5), and airline association.

  • 2,500 feedback events
  • Types: Complaint, Compliment, Claim, Survey
  • Satisfaction scale: 1–5
  • Airline association
