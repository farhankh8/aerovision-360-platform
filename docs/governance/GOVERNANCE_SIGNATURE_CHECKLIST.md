## 1. Signature Checklist Purpose

This checklist guides the project owner through the formal signature and approval process for all outstanding governance decisions. It ensures that:

- Every decision has a clear, explicit scope.
- Every decision has an explicit accept/reject/return choice.
- Every signature is dated and not backdated.
- Every decision records whether an independent reviewer was used or compensating controls were accepted.
- Every decision records residual concerns openly.
- Every decision records where the signed copy is retained and its hash.
- No decision is claimed as approved without verifiable evidence.

**No signature, date, reviewer identity, or approval reference is invented or pre-filled.** Every blank field is intentional and must be completed by the project owner at the time of signature.
